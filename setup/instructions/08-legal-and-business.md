# 08 · Legal & Business Basics

> ⚖️ **Not legal advice.** This is a friendly checklist of the common things a
> small online art shop sets up. For anything you're unsure about (taxes,
> business structure), a quick chat with a local accountant or the state's small
> business office is worth it.

Because the site collects names, addresses, and payment confirmations, you should
have a couple of simple policy pages and a few business basics in place.

---

## 1. Business basics

- [ ] **Business name** — decided in [Branding](06-branding.md). Optionally check
      your state's business registry to see if the name is taken.
- [ ] **Sole proprietor vs. LLC** — many artists start as a **sole proprietor**
      (nothing to file; you just operate). An **LLC** adds liability protection
      for ~$50–500 depending on the state. Fine to start simple and form an LLC
      later as sales grow.
- [ ] **Business bank account / payment accounts** — keep business money separate
      from personal. PayPal **Business** and Cash App **Business** help here.
- [ ] **Sales tax** — selling physical goods (canvases) can require collecting
      sales tax depending on your state and volume. Since orders are placed
      manually, you can handle this case by case at first; check your state's
      threshold. An accountant can set you straight in one conversation.
- [ ] **Income records** — keep the order log (see
      [Fulfillment](07-fulfillment-pictorem.md)) for tax time.

---

## 2. Policy pages you should publish

Add two simple pages: a **Privacy Policy** and a **Refund/Shipping Policy**.
Templates below — fill in the brackets `[ ]`. You can paste these into new
`privacy.html` / `terms.html` pages (ask your maintainer to create them styled
like the rest of the site), or host them anywhere and link from the footer.

### Privacy Policy (template)
```
PRIVACY POLICY — [Business Name]
Last updated: [date]

[Business Name] ("we") sells photographic canvas prints. This policy explains
what we collect and why.

What we collect:
- When you place an order, we collect your name, email, phone, shipping address,
  chosen artwork and size, and your payment confirmation (a transaction ID or
  screenshot). This information is sent to us by email so we can fulfill your
  order.
- We do NOT collect or store credit/debit card numbers. Payments are made
  directly through PayPal or Cash App, which have their own privacy policies.

How we use it:
- Only to process, fulfill, and ship your order, and to contact you about it.
- We use [Pictorem] to print and ship your canvas; we share your shipping address
  with them for delivery.

What we don't do:
- We don't sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Data retention:
- We keep order records as needed for fulfillment, customer service, and tax
  purposes.

Your choices:
- Email us at [contact email] to ask what we have or to request deletion.

Contact: [contact email]
```

### Refund & Shipping Policy (template)
```
SHIPPING & RETURNS — [Business Name]
Last updated: [date]

Made to order:
- Every canvas is custom-printed to order through our print partner. Production
  typically takes [X] business days, plus shipping time.

Shipping:
- We ship to the address provided at checkout. Shipping costs and timing are
  confirmed per order. You'll receive a confirmation when your piece enters
  production.

Cancellations:
- Because each piece is made to order, orders generally cannot be cancelled once
  production has begun. Contact us as soon as possible at [contact email] if you
  need to make a change.

Damaged or defective items:
- If your canvas arrives damaged or defective, contact us at [contact email]
  within [7] days with photos and we'll arrange a replacement.

Returns:
- As custom-made items, prints are not returnable except in the case of damage or
  defect described above.

Contact: [contact email]
```

> ✅ Link both pages in the website footer (the footer already has a "Support"
> column — these fit naturally next to FAQ).

## 3. Consumer-trust touches (optional but nice)

- A short **"About the artist"** story (the About page is ready for this).
- Clear **made-to-order timing** on the product/FAQ pages (already mentioned).
- A reachable **contact email** that's actually monitored.
- Real **Instagram** presence linked in the footer (builds trust fast).
