# 09 · Going Live

The fun part. Before you tell the world, run this pre-flight checklist so the
first real customer has a perfect experience.

---

## Pre-launch checklist

### Content
- [ ] Real **photos** added and look good in the gallery → [04](04-photos-and-catalog.md)
- [ ] **Titles, collections, tags** read nicely (no leftover filenames like `IMG_1234`)
- [ ] Best pieces marked `"featured": true` so the home page shines
- [ ] `npm run validate` passes with no errors

### Settings (`config.js`)
- [ ] **Business name & tagline** set
- [ ] **Fulfillment email** set to the brother's real inbox
- [ ] **PayPal handle** and **Cash App $Cashtag** are correct
- [ ] **Pricing** reviewed — open a few product pages and sanity-check the prices
- [ ] **Contact email** and **Instagram** set

### Orders & email
- [ ] Order delivery set up (EmailJS keys filled in **or** FormSubmit chosen) → [02](02-email-and-orders.md)
- [ ] If FormSubmit: completed the **one-time activation** email
- [ ] Contact form delivers to the right inbox

### Legal
- [ ] **Privacy** and **Refund/Shipping** policies published and linked → [08](08-legal-and-business.md)

### Hosting & domain
- [ ] Site deployed and loads at its host URL → [01](01-domain-and-hosting.md)
- [ ] Custom **domain connected** and showing the padlock 🔒 (HTTPS)
- [ ] `www.yourdomain.com` and `yourdomain.com` both work

---

## 🧪 The all-important test order

Do a **complete, real end-to-end order yourself** before launch:

1. Open the live site on your **phone** and on a **computer**.
2. Browse the gallery → open a photo → pick a size → click **Order**.
3. Fill in the form with your real details.
4. Choose PayPal, tap the pay button — confirm it opens **pre-filled with the
   correct amount** to **your** account.
5. Actually send a **small real payment** (you're paying yourself).
6. Add the transaction ID / screenshot and **submit**.
7. ✅ Confirm the **order email arrives** in the fulfillment inbox with all the
   details and the payment proof.
8. Walk the brother through **finding that payment** and doing a **test Pictorem
   order** (you can order a small canvas to keep as a sample — great for photos!).
9. Submit the **contact form** too and confirm that email arrives.

If every step works, you're ready. 🎉

---

## Cross-device & browser check

Open the site and click around on:
- [ ] iPhone / Android phone (most customers will be on mobile)
- [ ] A laptop/desktop
- [ ] At least two browsers (Chrome + Safari, or Chrome + Firefox)
- [ ] Try the **light/dark theme** toggle
- [ ] Try **search** and a couple of **collection filters**
- [ ] Open a photo's **lightbox** (click an image in the gallery)

---

## Launch day

1. **Soft launch:** share with a few friends/family first. Ask them to actually
   try ordering (or at least browse) and report anything confusing.
2. Fix anything they trip on.
3. **Announce:** post on Instagram, tell your circles, email contacts. See
   [11 · Growth & Marketing](11-growth-and-marketing.md).
4. Watch the fulfillment inbox for the first real orders and run the
   [fulfillment routine](07-fulfillment-pictorem.md).
