# 07 · Fulfillment (Pictorem) — the Brother's Guide

This page is for **the brother**, who turns each order email into a printed,
shipped canvas. Print this out or bookmark it.

---

## Your job in one sentence

> When an order email arrives, **confirm the customer paid**, then **place the
> matching canvas order on Pictorem** to ship directly to the customer.

---

## One-time setup

1. Create an account at [pictorem.com](https://www.pictorem.com).
2. Add a payment method (you pay Pictorem; the customer's money is already in the
   PayPal/Cash App account, which covers it plus profit).
3. Get familiar with their **canvas products** and **size options**, so the sizes
   on the website match what you can actually order.
4. Make sure you can access:
   - the **fulfillment email inbox** (where orders land)
   - the **PayPal** and **Cash App** accounts (to verify payments)
   - the **photo files** (the website links the exact photo in each order; for
     printing you'll upload the full-resolution original — keep a shared folder of
     originals, e.g. Google Drive/Dropbox).

---

## The routine for every order

### 1. Read the order email
It contains everything: photo ID & title, **canvas size**, total, customer
contact, **shipping address**, payment method, and the **transaction ID /
screenshot**.

### 2. ✅ Verify the payment FIRST (most important step)
- Open PayPal or Cash App.
- Find the payment that matches the **amount** and the **customer's name** in the
  order.
- Confirm it actually **cleared** (not pending/disputed).
- ❌ If you can't find the payment, **do not print.** Email the customer using the
  address in the order to sort it out.

### 3. Place the order on Pictorem
1. Find/upload the **full-resolution original** of the ordered photo (match it by
   the photo **ID/title** in the email).
2. Choose **Canvas**, gallery-wrapped, **1.5″** depth (or whatever your site
   promises in the product "finishes").
3. Select the **exact size** from the order (e.g. `16 x 24 in`).
4. Set the **shipping address** to the **customer's** address from the email
   (drop-ship straight to them).
5. Review and pay. Save the Pictorem **order/tracking number**.

### 4. Confirm with the customer (recommended)
Reply to the customer's email:
> "Thanks for your order! Your *[photo title]* canvas (16×24) is now in
> production and will ship to [address]. We'll share tracking when it's available."

### 5. Keep a simple log
A spreadsheet with: date, customer, photo, size, amount paid, Pictorem order #,
tracking #, status. This makes questions and taxes painless later.

## Handling the tricky cases

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Payment not found | Don't print. Contact the customer; wait until it clears. |
| Wrong/blurry screenshot | Ask the customer for the transaction ID or a clearer image. |
| Customer wants a custom size | Coordinate via email; you can still order it on Pictorem manually. |
| Address looks wrong/incomplete | Confirm with the customer before printing. |
| Refund request | See the refund policy in [08 · Legal & Business](08-legal-and-business.md). Pictorem orders, once in production, usually can't be cancelled — set expectations. |
| Print arrives damaged | Pictorem handles reprints for defects/shipping damage — contact their support with photos. |

---

## Timing expectations to set

- **Verification:** same day you see the order, ideally.
- **Production + shipping:** Pictorem's stated turnaround (typically several
  business days) plus shipping time.
- Tell customers "made to order — please allow X days," so nobody expects
  next-day delivery.

---

## Why it's done this way

Manual fulfillment means **no automated print integration to build or pay for**,
full control over quality, and you only ever pay Pictorem **after** the customer
has paid you. It's a clean, low-risk way to run the shop.
